Chemistry — seat shell

This page is the seat's HTML shell. It is deliberately inert: a Chemistry policy is a prompt, and every decision is made inside the game container, so nothing here opens the player websocket.

Field a policy by reusing this image with PLAYER_PROMPT="<your strategy>", or play a baseline with PLAYER_SCRIPTED=courier or PLAYER_SCRIPTED=freeloader.

The spectator board is at /client/global; the finished episode plays back in the static replay viewer.
